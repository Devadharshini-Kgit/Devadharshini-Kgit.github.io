Bank Customer Churn Analysis

SQLPythonPower BIExcel

Question: which customers are leaving, and who should retention target first?

  • SQL CTE pipelines over 10,000+ bank records found the highest-risk groups: age 46-60, Germany and high-balance customers.
  • K-Means clustering split customers into VIP (307), At-Risk (377) and Passive (316) cohorts for retention priority.
  • ETL-fed Power BI and Excel dashboards cover all three segments for MIS reporting.
  • Recommendation: targeted retention for ages 46-60, region-specific engagement for Germany, loyalty offers for high-balance customers.

Customer Funnel and Retention

PythonStatisticsA/B testing

Question: where do users drop off, do they come back, and does a simpler checkout help?

View to Cart drop-off62.1%

  • Overall conversion from view to purchase is 15.8%.
  • Month-1 cohort retention sits around 30-40%, which marks the first month after purchase as the re-engagement window.
